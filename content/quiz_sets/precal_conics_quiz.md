### Question 1 (mcq)
What is the radius and center of the circle with standard equation $(x - 3)^2 + (y + 5)^2 = 49$?

- [x] Center $(3, -5)$, Radius $r = 7$
- [ ] Center $(-3, 5)$, Radius $r = 7$
- [ ] Center $(3, -5)$, Radius $r = 49$
- [ ] Center $(-3, 5)$, Radius $r = 49$

> **Explanation:** The standard circle equation is $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$. Comparing values: $h = 3$, $k = -5$, and $r = \sqrt{49} = 7$.

### Question 2 (mcq)
Find the foci of the hyperbola:
$$ \frac{(x - 2)^2}{16} - \frac{(y + 1)^2}{9} = 1 $$

- [ ] $(2 \pm \sqrt{7}, -1)$
- [x] $(2 \pm 5, -1)$
- [ ] $(2, -1 \pm 5)$
- [ ] $(2 \pm 25, -1)$

> **Explanation:** For a horizontal hyperbola, $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$. Here $a^2 = 16$ and $b^2 = 9$, so $c^2 = 25 \implies c = 5$. The foci are $(h \pm c, k) = (2 \pm 5, -1)$.

### Question 3 (numerical)
A horizontal parabola has equation $(y - 2)^2 = 16(x + 1)$. What is the focal distance parameter $p$?

correctValue: 4
tolerance: 0.01

> **Explanation:** The standard form is $(y - k)^2 = 4p(x - h)$. Matching $4p = 16 \implies p = 4$.
