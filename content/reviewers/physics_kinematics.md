### Key Kinematics Formulas:

For uniform accelerated linear motion:
- Final Velocity: $ v = v_0 + at $
- Position: $ \Delta x = v_0 t + \frac{1}{2}at^2 $
- Timeless Equation: $ v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a\Delta x $

### 2D Projectile Motion:
Horizontal component (constant velocity):
$$ v_x = v_0 \cdot \cos\theta $$

Vertical component (accelerated by gravity $g = 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2$):
$$ v_y = v_0 \cdot \sin\theta - gt $$

### Projectile Range & Max Height:
- **Maximum Range:** $$ R = \frac{v_0^2 \cdot \sin(2\theta)}{g} $$
- **Maximum Height:** $$ H = \frac{v_0^2 \cdot \sin^2\theta}{2g} $$
