### Conic Sections Standard Equations

#### 1. Circle:
$$ (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2 $$
- Center at $(h, k)$ with radius $r$.

#### 2. Parabola:
- **Vertical Axis:** $(x - h)^2 = 4p(y - k)$, Focus at $(h, k + p)$
- **Horizontal Axis:** $(y - k)^2 = 4p(x - h)$, Focus at $(h + p, k)$

#### 3. Ellipse (Horizontal Major Axis):
$$ \frac{(x - h)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y - k)^2}{b^2} = 1 $$
- Where $c^2 = a^2 - b^2$, and foci located at $(h \pm c, k)$.

#### 4. Hyperbola (Horizontal Transverse Axis):
$$ \frac{(x - h)^2}{a^2} - \frac{(y - k)^2}{b^2} = 1 $$
- Where $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$, and asymptotes given by:
$$ y - k = \pm \frac{b}{a}(x - h) $$
