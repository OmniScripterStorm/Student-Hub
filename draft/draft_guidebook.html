A message from your Grade 11 Representative

To my fellow Scientians and dearest batchmates,

When our school first opened its doors to our batch in Grade 7, the student handbook welcomed us with an invitation that still echoes today: “So, get involved! THIS IS YOUR SCHOOL.”

Those words remain just as true now as they were then.

To my fellow old students who have walked with me for years: you already know the way of life, our traditions, and what it means to be a Scientian. Thank you for continuing to bear the values of Excellence, Servitude, and Honesty.

To our transferees: a warm welcome to Taguig Science High School. Stepping into a new school is never easy, but you will always have a place here. From day one, this is your home, and this is your school.

Senior High School brings tougher challenges and bigger responsibilities. This Guidebook and the Student Hub alongside it were created so that all of us have the tools, voice, and support to succeed together.

Let us learn from one another, lift each other up, and lead the road to excellence as one.

Hail to TagSci, the Home of Champions

Scientian Protocols

Code of Conduct

Hallway Decorum

  • Maintain conversational and quiet volume at all times when moving between classrooms, especially near ongoing junior high classes and specialized laboratories.
  • Keep stairwells, fire exits, and central hallway corridors unobstructed. Avoid loitering or sitting in large clusters along corridor floors during transitions or vacant periods.
  • Trash bins are placed in strategic places around the halls, there should be no excuse for littering.

Punctuality

  • Students are expected to be on campus premises at least 15 minutes before the official start of the first instructional period.
  • Students arriving after the designated entry cutoff must head to the Guidance Office and fill out necessary paperwork before entering their classroom.

School Identification and Campus Security

  • The ID must be visible, properly worn, and identifiable. In case of loss, report to the class adviser.

Science Lab Conduct

  • After use, make sure to return the seats under the table, return all lab material/tools used, and refrain from littering.

Personal Decorum

  • Boys' haircuts must not cover the ears and eyebrows. Girls may choose appropriate hairstyles and should see to it that it is neat and tidy. Dying of hair should be limited to black and dark brown.
  • Girls should only wear one pair of earrings.
  • Body tattoos are forbidden.
  • These protocols are usually enforced:
    • • Before a major exam (Summative, Term Exam)
    • • At random

Cellular Device

  • Use of phones in class is prohibited, unless required by the subject teacher as part of the lesson.
